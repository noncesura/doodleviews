doodleviews

Views of six English Wikipedia articles around the day each one got a Google Doodle in 2013: Celia Cruz, Shakuntala Devi, Raymond Loewy, Hermann Rorschach, Maria Callas and Grace Hopper.

Disclaimers about this data:

  • The data is taken from pagecounts-ez. It only counts desktop views. There is no filter for bots, spiders or crawlers.
  • Times on this data are in UTC.
  • "No data" means the dump didn't record any data for that hour. It doesn't mean zero views.
